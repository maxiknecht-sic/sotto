The one thing nobody curated

The wine is chosen. The caviar is sourced. The menu is built by someone who cares about it. Then the coffee arrives from a pod machine, or from a flask that has been standing since breakfast.

It is the only thing on the table that no one considered, and the thing your guests will ask for most often across a long day.

In a five-star hotel this has usually been solved. On a deck, on a villa terrace, in a hospitality suite or at a private residence, it has not.

What we actually do

One exceptional coffee, dialled by hand and held there all day.

Decaf on a second grinder, because at a late dinner someone always asks. Batch brew for volume. Milk drinks made properly and poured properly, in glassware that belongs on your table.

No syrup rack, no flavour list, no menu board. Your guests are not choosing between fourteen drinks. They are being handed the best cup they have had all week, made in front of them.
